# LumenWealth Client Channels Guide

> Fictional sample document created for the Agentic RAG demo. LumenWealth is not a real product.

## 1. Available channels

Clients can access their accounts through three channels:

| Channel | What clients can do |
|---|---|
| **Web portal** | View portfolios, download statements, send secure messages, sign documents |
| **Mobile app** (iOS and Android) | View portfolios, approve payments, receive notifications |
| **Relationship manager** | Orders, advice, onboarding and any request not available digitally |

Clients cannot place trading orders through the mobile app. Trading is available on the web portal for execution-only clients, and through the relationship manager for advisory clients.

## 2. Enrolling a client in digital channels

1. In the Advisor Workstation, open the client and go to **Channels**.
2. Click **Enable Digital Access** and confirm the client's mobile number and email address.
3. The client receives an activation email valid for **72 hours**.
4. The client sets a password and registers the mobile app as their second factor.

If the activation link expires, the RM can resend it from the same screen. A new link cancels any earlier one.

## 3. Statements and documents

- Monthly portfolio statements are published on the **5th business day** of each month.
- Annual tax reports are published by **31 March** for the previous year.
- Documents stay available in the portal for 10 years.
- Clients can switch off paper statements under **Settings > Documents**.

## 4. Secure messaging

Clients can send messages to their RM from the portal or app. RMs must reply within **2 business days**. Messages cannot be used to place orders or change payment instructions; those requests must be confirmed by phone.

## 5. Locked accounts

A client account locks after **5 failed sign-in attempts**. To unlock it:

1. Verify the client's identity by phone using two security questions.
2. In the Advisor Workstation, go to **Channels > Unlock Access**.
3. The client receives a new activation email.

## 6. Mobile app requirements

The app supports the two most recent major versions of iOS and Android. Rooted or jailbroken devices are blocked for security reasons.
